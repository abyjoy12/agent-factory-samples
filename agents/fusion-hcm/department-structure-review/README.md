## Evidence and limits

Resource: `/hcmRestApi/resources/11.13.18.05/organizations`

Projection: `OrganizationId,Name,Status,EffectiveStartDate,EffectiveEndDate`

Treat the resource as department definitions, not employee membership or an organizational hierarchy. Do not infer manager relationships or staffing counts.

Request `limit=10` and `onlyData=true`, do not expand child resources, and display at most five records. Query limits and projections are instructions; use credential scope or a gateway for hard enforcement. A sample is not the full population. No application changes are made.
